World quality pass 1 audit

Run `node tests/world-audit.mjs after` from the repository root. The runner owns a server on a free port in 18080–18099, uses a new `/tmp/jhp-world-audit-*` directory and removes it on completion. It registers an existing/full-room account through the admin fixture and signs up a separate sparse player. It never reads production saves. No dependencies are added.

`tests/shots/world-audit/<tag>/full.json` and `sparse.json` contain ranked source locations, screenshots, actual coplanar triangle intersections (0.02 cm tolerance), exact support contacts, and camera results. Mesh-builder source tracing is opt-in through the throwaway browser's `jhp_world_audit` flag; it survives static batching. Ordinary play does not collect stacks.

The camera scan samples collision-valid positions on a 10 cm grid, both standing/crouched eye heights, 16 horizontal directions and two vertical directions. Near-plane clearance includes the projection rectangle's farthest corner. It tests closed and fully open doors. Vertical coverage checks reach 600 cm; ordinary clearance rays reach 81 cm. Exact triangle-edge hits are counted separately because either adjoining face may win a floating-point tie. Collision-valid sampling is not evidence of a continuously walked route.

Temporary console-carton and CRT-sized props exercise the real grab system through four wall/stair poses and 16 yaw angles. These are diagnostic solids: the installed PC monitor and the decorative Daydream carton are not currently grabbable. They are not added to production content or real account state. Samples test settled poses, not every animation frame or every held-object/world intersection.

Every reported intersection links to a room overview; the highest 24 source clusters per account also get close-ups. Fixed before/after views cover the plate, stair edge, shared door jamb and canopy; carry captures cover two opposing angles. Additional 1920×1080 views inspect each corrected area from another direction. This is not an individual close-up of every candidate. Room overviews plus grouped source locations make the unreviewed remainder explicit.

Coplanar candidates include buried joins and identical-material seams. Closed structural panels add internal faces and therefore increase raw candidate counts; a higher count alone is not a visual regression. Curved/non-axis-aligned coplanarity, transparent glazing, instanced grass and intentional contact shadows are outside the overlap scan. Small scenery gaps between samples and beyond the local ray range still need manual inspection. Static batch undersides also have a separate `staticContacts` list of opposing horizontal faces; these are normally hidden support joins. The `contacts` list measures exact whole-object bottoms for grab-system props.

The first closed-scene comparison found 58 exact support contacts on the full account and none after the shared render lift, including the floor plate. The original scene also exposed the garden canopy, parked mower handle, stair margin and rear closed-door corridor. Fixes preserve logical prop/save coordinates; the small clearance is applied only to their rendered poses. The hall is visibly carpeted, so its footsteps use carpet rather than the available tile profile.

For baseline capture after source edits, copy the original `hs-world.js`, `hs-house.js`, `rm-room.js` and `ch-out.js` into a temporary directory, then run `WORLD_AUDIT_SOURCE_DIR=/tmp/<directory> node tests/world-audit.mjs before`. This browser-only route override leaves the working files intact. `--views-only` repeats fixed/carry captures without replacing the geometry reports. Test logs and captures are ignored artifacts under `tests/shots/world-audit/`.

The sound unit exercises surface profiles, weight profiles, action sounds, pause/resume and effects/music/volume isolation. It cannot judge loudspeaker balance or subjective timbre. Hardware/gamepad testing and a complete manual sweep of every furniture animation remain outside this pass's automated coverage.

The cupboard now has a collision-valid entrance behind its door. The regression uses F, walking and a jump into it, then refreshes with deliberately rounded legacy coordinates. The low ceiling limits jump height; pose saving retains precise horizontal coordinates, and legacy half-millimetre boundary errors recover inside the same room. `--walk-only` runs that focused check; `--camera-only` skips the expensive overlap list while retaining camera/coverage checks. Tags beginning with `after` fail on camera/coverage findings, exact loose-prop contacts or camera entry into carried items. Raw coplanar/static-support candidates remain review items rather than automatic proof of flicker.

Recorded final results are in `shots/world-audit/after-pass1/{full,sparse}.json`. The full account sampled 5,462 closed-door and 5,526 open-door cells across all eight rooms, including the cupboard. Both profiles have no sampled near-plane/back-face/coverage findings, no exact loose-prop support contacts and no camera entry in 128 carry samples each. The cupboard legacy-save refresh recovered x385 to x384.949 in the same room.

The remaining full-account lists contain 543 coplanar source/plane groups and 388 opposing static-support groups; the sparse account has 429 and 369 respectively. These include hidden construction joints and are not counts of confirmed visible defects. Each list is retained with source locations and capture links for further manual review. Do not claim the entire world is free of defects from this sampled pass.

The shared lift measures fine underside details up to 5 mm after updating the complete model transform hierarchy. Larger soft-model anchors keep their original convention. The beanbag has a closed, flat contact base, and the crumpled paper aligns its lowest vertex to its support. Saved logical prop positions remain unchanged. The pose unit additionally checks exact resume, old rounded saves, bounded same-room recovery from new door margins and rejection of unsafe/non-finite poses.

Run profiles separately on slow software-rendering hosts: `node tests/world-audit.mjs after-pass1 --account=full`, then `node tests/world-audit.mjs after-pass1 --account=sparse`. This retains both reports under one capture tag while keeping each process shorter. Earlier intermediate tags are diagnostic history; `after-pass1` contains the final geometry captures.
