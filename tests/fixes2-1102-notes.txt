JackWorld startup furniture check

Cause: server.js initialises mods in alphabetical order. jw.js calls loot.api.P
before loot.js init has assigned its module-local D. P immediately reads D.p,
throwing the reported TypeError. The single catch encloses every room, so the
first eligible owner's room aborts the rest of the check.

Before: for rooms whose owner still had a member record, filter each placement
against that owner's chest. An unlocked matching item acquired lk='jw'; a
missing item, or an item with another lock, lost its room placement. Deleted
owners' rooms were skipped. When an exception occurred inside a filter, that
room's replacement array was not assigned, but earlier lock mutations and any
earlier completed room filters could remain. On the confirmed cold startup,
the first loot.P call throws before filtering that first eligible room.

After: read existing saved loot records directly, independent of mod init order.
Never create a chest and never filter/rewrite a room or placement at startup.
Only an unambiguous positive integer item UID with a known private furniture
ID matching its saved placement, owned by the existing room owner, can acquire
a missing jw lock. Missing/malformed records, unknown IDs, duplicates and other
locks are skipped and preserved. Later valid records still get checked.
Existing correctly locked rooms and all room placements are unchanged. A valid
unlocked placed item's lock is the only intended persisted change (markDirty).
Unresolved stale references remain for explicit gameplay handling; startup
does not guess whether missing data means a trade or a damaged save.
No live state or data/ was opened, copied or modified for this task.
