### World quality pass 2 (branch `world-quality-2`, 2026-10-26)

Root causes, fixed once in the shared kit so every builder benefits:
- [B] `hs-kit.js` `roundBox`/`rbox`: a real bevel (flat faces, grid placed at the band edges, rounded or chamfered band of radius r; per-face materials). The old version spread the bevel over the whole face, smearing normals; the new one is also cheaper (108 or 300 triangles instead of 588). `K.doorLeaf` builds moulded four-panel leaves with lever handles and hinges, merged per material.
- [C] `B.shadow`/`B.wshadow`: flat dark core over the footprint, fade only over the outer margin (nine-slice or ring geometry), so contact shadows reach the object's edge at any size. Merged shadows are flagged like single ones (hidden while carried, never picked). Grab props with no shadow of their own get one instanced shadow per room in `hs-world.js` (one draw call per room).
- [D] Surface textures carry their real tile size (`userData.worldUV`); any material using them gets world-space planar UVs per face (bevel bands included), with wood grain turned along each face's long side.
- [A] Walls, carpets, woods, lino and wallpaper render at 2x with full-resolution pile/grain/tooth added in one pixel pass; small print (timetable, calendar, notes, photo, labels) at 2x; Low gets 2x anisotropic filtering; `setAniso(a, true)` re-filters existing textures on a deliberate graphics change.
- [E] HUD: toasts start below the tin/diary column, Mom's shouts sit in the lower third, solid minimap backing; the live TV bar is one row under the bezel.
