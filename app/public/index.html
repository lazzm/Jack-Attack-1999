      Hobby Corner
      PAINT & GLUE
      
  
      
    
      What's on my desk this month
      
    
      When I'm not playing the N64 I'm making stuff. Right now on my desk: an Airfix Spitfire with one wing on, a K'Nex rollercoaster that goes round once then falls over, a box of LEGO spacemen, a Micro Machines car that lives in the carpet, and a squad of Space Marines halfway through being painted blue. Mom says my bedroom smells of polystyrene cement. She is right.
      
    
      I paint Warhammer miniatures too: Space Marines, Eldar, a Chaos lord Callum gave me, and my favourites, the Orks (big, green and LOUD, they think red ones go faster). There's a bits box under my bed that's slowly taking over the bedroom.
      
    
      Jack's top painting tips
      
    
      1. Undercoat first (black hides mistakes, white makes colours bright). 2. Thin your paints with a bit of water. 3. Don't lick the brush. 4. Drybrushing makes ANYTHING look good. 5. Keep the cat away from the glue.

        Drybrushing — Wiping most of the paint off, then painting. It works. Nobody knows why.
      
        Sprue — The plastic frame the bits come on. Never throw it away (you will).
